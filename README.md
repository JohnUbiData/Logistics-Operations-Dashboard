🚚 Logistics Operations Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing logistics operations, fleet performance, costs, safety, and delivery performance.

📊 Project Overview

The Logistics Operations Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive view of logistics and fleet performance. It brings together key operational, financial, safety, and delivery metrics to help identify performance trends, monitor costs, and highlight areas that may require attention.

The dashboard analyzes metrics including revenue, fuel costs, miles driven, fleet utilization, safety incidents, injury incidents, and on-time delivery performance. It is designed to give decision makers a clearer view of operational efficiency and support data-driven decisions across the logistics operation.

🎯 Business Questions

* How much revenue is the logistics operation generating?
* What is the relationship between fuel costs and overall revenue?
* How efficiently is the fleet being utilized?
* How many miles are being covered and how does fleet activity change over time?
* What is the level of safety performance across the operation?
* How frequently are safety and injury incidents occurring?
* How well is the operation performing against its delivery targets?
* What factors may be contributing to delays and operational inefficiencies?

🛠️ Tools Used

* Power BI — Data visualization and dashboard development
* Power Query — Data cleaning and transformation
* DAX — Measures and KPI calculations

On-Time Delivery	55.7%

💡 Key Insights

* Strong revenue generation: The operation generated $263M in total revenue, indicating significant business activity across the fleet.
* Fuel represents a significant operating cost: With $96M spent on fuel, fuel efficiency and consumption management are important areas for controlling operating costs.
* High fleet activity: The fleet covered 122M miles, reflecting substantial vehicle utilization and operational activity.
* Safety requires attention: The dashboard recorded 170 safety incidents, including 33 injury incidents, highlighting the importance of strengthening safety measures and monitoring incident patterns.
* Delivery performance has room for improvement: With an on-time delivery rate of 55.7%, only slightly more than half of deliveries were completed on schedule. This suggests an opportunity to investigate the operational factors contributing to delays.
* Operational efficiency is closely connected: Revenue generation, fuel expenditure, fleet utilization, safety, and delivery performance should be monitored together to identify opportunities to improve overall logistics efficiency and profitability.

📌 Recommendations

* Improve fuel efficiency: Monitor fuel consumption and fuel cost per mile across vehicles to identify inefficient vehicles and opportunities to reduce fuel expenditure.
* Optimize fleet utilization: Track vehicle utilization, mileage, and downtime to identify underutilized assets and improve fleet productivity.
* Strengthen safety management: Analyze safety and injury incidents by vehicle, driver, location, or time period to identify recurring risk patterns and prioritize preventive measures.
* Improve on-time delivery: Investigate the operational factors behind delayed deliveries and use delivery performance data to improve route planning, scheduling, and resource allocation.
* Monitor operational costs: Track fuel and maintenance costs alongside revenue to identify cost trends and protect overall profitability.
* Use performance trends for continuous improvement: Establish regular monitoring of key operational KPIs to identify emerging issues early and measure the impact of improvement initiatives.
